## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative yet welcoming institutional foundation for a tripartite educational environment: School Principals (operational oversight, systemic health, compliance), Teachers (curriculum pacing, pedagogical logging, intervention tracking), and Parents/Students (academic progress, scheduling, direct transparency).

The aesthetic marries **Corporate/Modern** rigor with intentional humanist warmth. It eschews sterile academic databases in favor of structured, editorial clarity: crisp visual hierarchy, tactile card surfaces, purposeful data-ink ratios, and focused visual telemetry (micro-progress rings, milestone badges, status flags). 

The emotional response must convey:
- **Quiet Institutional Confidence:** Reassuring leadership with structured data governance.
- **Cognitive Relief for Educators:** Reducing daily friction through modular, glanceable curriculum matrices.
- **Approachable Agency for Families:** Eliminating academic anxiety through luminous, comprehensible progress tracking.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative gravitas with tactical vibrancy. Deep Slate (`#1E293B`) anchors primary commands and high-level institutional structures. Vivid Indigo (`#2563EB`) acts as the active behavioral driver for actions, selections, and interactive orchestrations. Sage Emerald (`#059669`) celebrates mastery, completed syllabi, and positive evaluations, while Warm Amber (`#D97706`) isolates pedagogical bottlenecks, impending exam deadliness, and parental alerts without inducing panic.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary / Institutional Base (`#1E293B`):** System navigation, administrative headers, critical typography, structural delimiters.
- **Secondary / Interaction Engine (`#2563EB`):** Focused states, action buttons, dynamic timeline progressors, active syllabus nodes.
- **Tertiary / Mastery Accent (`#059669`):** Competency completions, positive attendance deltas, validated curriculum milestones.
- **Warning & Exam Accent (`#D97706`):** Assessment checkpoints, submission warnings, remediation flags.
- **Surface Foundations:** Primary canvas is anchored at `#F8FAFC`, with layered interactive modules elevated in pure `#FFFFFF`. Subtle borders utilize `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent administrative titles, analytical banners, and milestone scores. Its subtle geometric warmth prevents institutional fatigue while keeping headers crisp and confident.

**Inter** handles all operational workflows, syllabus trees, performance metrics, and dense table views. It provides neutral, mathematically sound legibility across high-density data matrices.

### Rules of Usage
- **Numerics & Tabular Metrics:** All grades, pacing percentages, and attendance figures rendered in Inter must apply tabular lining (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve alignment across dashboards.
- **Labels & Badges:** Micro-labels, term indicators, and stage progressors rely on `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight letter spacing to reinforce scannability in multi-column tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system across desktop views, collapsing gracefully to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Form-Factor Strategies
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables side-by-side comparison of multi-term curriculum roadmaps, institutional KPIs, and split-pane lesson builders.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Prioritizes responsive dual-pane views: teacher grading queues and student calendar lists.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid stack with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Bottom navigation becomes primary; complex matrices distill into sequential swipeable cards and vertical milestone steppers.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm follows an 8px base unit. Internal component padding adheres strictly to the `space-*` tokens: compact controls use `space-sm` (8px), interactive cards leverage `space-lg` (24px), and structural section gaps scale up to `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **tonal layering** and **low-contrast outlines**, eschewing heavy, distracting dropshadows to preserve focus on complex curriculum structures.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The structural viewport foundation behind all workspaces.
- **Surface Neutral (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority operational cards, content panes, data tables, and modal dialogs.
- **Surface Muted (`#F1F5F9`):** Inactive track elements, secondary curriculum blocks, and table header rows.

### Shadow & Boundary Tokens
- **Borders as Structure:** Primary containment relies on a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border rather than heavy elevation.
- **Elevated Level 1 (Resting Cards):** `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Elevated Level 2 (Active / Hover / Dropdown):** `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Elevated Level 3 (Modals / Intervention Drawers):** `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, setting standard interactives and containers to `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This provides an approachable, contemporary visual softness without undermining professional institutional discipline.

### Geometry Breakdown
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Micro-Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px). Maintains structural alignment with typography baselines.
- **Container Elements (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) applied to curriculum phase containers, metric summary cards, and assignment wrappers.
- **High-Order Flyouts (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for contextual modals, diagnostic overlays, and bottom sheets on mobile.
- **Status Pills & Progress Tracks:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for completion badges, status tags, and progress ring caps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E293B` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#0F172A`. Focused state exhibits a `2px` offset outline in `#2563EB`.
- **Action (Pedagogical Drivers):** Solid `#2563EB` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for publishing lesson plans, submitting grades, and approving curricula.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, text `#64748B`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Refined Badges
- **Completed / Syllabus Mastered:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, subtle `1px solid #A7F3D0` border.
- **Exam / Pending Alert:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text, subtle `1px solid #FDE68A` border.
- **Administrative / Role:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#2563EB` text, subtle `1px solid #BFDBFE` border.
- All chips maintain `rounded-full` (pill) styling, utilizing `label-sm` with vertical padding of `0.25rem` and horizontal padding of `0.625rem`.

### Micro-Progress Rings & Pacing Meters
- Circular SVG indicators with a track stroke of `#E2E8F0` and dynamic status strokes: `#059669` for on-track/mastered, `#2563EB` for active pacing, and `#D97706` for remediation.
- Rings contain centered tabular percentages using `label-sm` or `title-sm`.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Text rendered in `body-md`.
- Focus state shifts border color to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`).

### Cards & Module Panes
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, border-radius `1rem`. Internal padding conforms to `space-lg` (24px).
- Persona-specific header accents: Principal cards feature a left structural rule in `#1E293B`; Teacher lesson nodes highlight in `#2563EB`; Student achievement containers utilize `#059669`.

### Lists & Curriculum Orchestration Trees
- Rows separated by thin `1px solid #F1F5F9` dividers. Hover states on interactive syllabus nodes produce `#F8FAFC` backgrounds with a subtle `2px` left inset indicator matching the node's current status color.
